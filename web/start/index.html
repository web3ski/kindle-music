Kindle Music

Control Sonos speakers (and optionally Spotify) from a Kindle. Follow the steps in order. You can read this page on your phone or computer and type the commands on the Kindle, or open it in the Kindle's own browser.

Alpha. Jailbreaking can void your warranty and, in rare cases, brick the device. You do it at your own risk. Not affiliated with Amazon, Sonos, Spotify, IKEA or KindleModding.

Step 1: Check your Kindle

  1. On the Kindle open Settings → Device Options → Device Info and note the model and firmware version.
  2. Check that this model and firmware are supported by the jailbreak:

Jailbreak wizard (KindleModding)

Kindle Music needs a Kindle with Wi-Fi on the same network as your Sonos speakers.

Step 2: Jailbreak with SpiderCat (once)

Use the official SpiderCat guide from KindleModding. Follow it exactly, including when to turn airplane mode on or off. It installs the package manager (KPM) that the next step uses.

On the Kindle, open the browser and type this address in the address bar:

https://kindlemodding.org/spidercat

Open SpiderCat SpiderCat guide

We link to the official guide instead of copying it, so you always get the current steps. Already jailbroken with SpiderCat? Go to step 3.

Step 3: Install Kindle Music

Coming soon. Kindle Music is being submitted to the official KindleModding repository. Until it is listed there, the commands below will report that the package was not found. Advanced: install over SSH now.

On the Kindle home screen, tap the search bar, type the first line exactly and press Enter. Wait until it finishes, then do the next line.

;kpm update
;kpm install kindlemusic
  • Each line starts with a semicolon ;.
  • KOReader is installed automatically if you do not have it yet.
  • Text appears at the top of the screen for a moment and then the home screen comes back. That is normal.

Step 4: Open it

A Kindle Music item appears in your library. Tap it to open the remote. Speakers on the same Wi-Fi are found automatically.

Updates

;kpm update
;kpm upgrade

Something did not work?

  • Nothing happens after Enter: check that the line starts with ; and that the jailbreak (step 2) finished.
  • Package not found: run ;kpm update again, then ;kpm install kindlemusic. Until Kindle Music is in the official repository, use the SSH method below.
  • The search bar only accepts letters, digits, spaces and dashes, so commands with a web address (such as ;kpm add-repo) do not work from it.
  • Still stuck? Open an issue with the exact message shown on screen: github.com/web3ski/kindle-music/issues

Advanced: install over SSH

If your Kindle has SSH enabled (for example with Jobman), run this from a computer on the same Wi-Fi. Replace KINDLE_IP with your Kindle's IP address.

ssh root@KINDLE_IP '/var/local/kmc/bin/kpm add-repo https://web3ski.github.io/kindle-music/r && /var/local/kmc/bin/kpm update && /var/local/kmc/bin/kpm -y install kindlemusic'

After that, ;kpm update and ;kpm upgrade in the search bar keep it up to date.

Spotify (optional)

Lets you browse your playlists, search, start radio and like songs from the Kindle. You need Spotify Premium. Spotify only gives small projects access through your own free "developer app", so one person in the household creates it once (about 5 minutes, on a phone or computer).

A. Create your Spotify app (once per household)

Never done this? No problem: it's free, nothing gets published, and you don't need to know anything about programming. It works on a phone, and the Kindle's phone page (part B) walks you through the same steps.

  1. Open the Spotify developer website.

    Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard

    Tap Log in and sign in with the same Spotify account you use to listen to music.
  2. First time only: accept the terms. If Spotify shows Developer Terms of Service, tick the box and tap Accept the terms. If it asks you to verify your e-mail, open the e-mail from Spotify, tap the link in it and go back to the dashboard.
  3. Start a new app. On the dashboard tap Create app (on a phone you may need to scroll). A form called Create app opens.
  4. Name the app. App name: Kindle Music. App description: Remote for my Kindle. Website: leave it empty. The names don't matter; only you will see them.
  5. Add the Redirect URI. Tap Copy under the address below. In the Spotify form tap the Redirect URIs field, paste it and tap Add. The address appears in a list under the field. It must be exactly the same, including the slash at the end.
https://web3ski.github.io/kindle-music/callback/
  1. Tick Web API and save. Under Which API/SDKs are you planning to use? tick Web API and leave the others unticked. Tick I understand and agree with Spotify's Developer Terms of Service… and tap Save. The page of your new app opens.
  2. Other people in the household (optional). Only if someone else will link their own Spotify account: on the app's page tap Settings, then the User Management tab. Type their name and the e-mail of their Spotify account and tap Add user (up to 5 people).
  3. Copy the Client ID. On the app's page tap Settings. Under Basic Information you see Client ID, a code of 32 letters and digits. Copy it; you paste it in part B. You don't need the Client secret, and it should stay private.

B. Link a Spotify account to the Kindle

  1. On the Kindle open Kindle Music → Settings → Account → + Add account with your phone. A QR code appears.
  2. Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the Kindle, then scan the code with the phone's camera.
  3. On the page that opens, tap Yes, I have a Client ID, paste it and tap Sign in with Spotify. No Spotify app yet? Tap No, show me how and the phone guides you through part A.
  4. Log in with the Spotify account that should be used on this Kindle and tap Agree.
  5. The phone shows Linked and the Kindle shows the account name. Done.

To play Spotify through Sonos speakers, the same Spotify account must also be added in the Sonos app (Settings → Services & Voice → Add a service → Spotify). Several accounts can be linked; pick the active one under Settings → Account. Linked before version 0.2? Link the account again once, so the Like button gets its new permission.

  • "INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid redirect URI": the Redirect URI in your Spotify app is not exactly the address above (check the slash at the end). Fix it on the app's page under Settings → Edit.
  • The phone can't open the Kindle page: the phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the Kindle (not mobile data, not a guest network).
  • "User not registered in the Developer Dashboard": add that person in the app's User Management (step 7).
  • "Premium required" or nothing plays: the account that created the Spotify app needs Spotify Premium.
