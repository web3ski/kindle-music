Kindle Music

Control Sonos speakers from your Kindle. Open this page in the Kindle's own web browser and follow the steps.

Alpha. Jailbreaking can void your warranty and, in rare cases, brick the device. You do this at your own risk. Not affiliated with Amazon, Sonos, Spotify or IKEA.

Step 1: Jailbreak (once)

Check that your model and firmware are supported, turn on airplane mode only when the guide says so, then open the official SpiderCat page:

SpiderCat guide

Already jailbroken with SpiderCat? Skip to step 2.

Step 2: Install Kindle Music

On the Kindle home screen, tap the search bar, type each line exactly and press Enter. Wait for each one to finish.

;kpm add-repo web3ski.github.io/kindle-music/r ;kpm update ;kpm install koreader ;kpm install kindlemusic

Already have KOReader? You can skip the middle line.

Step 3: Open it

A Kindle Music item appears in your library. Tap it to open the remote. Speakers on the same Wi-Fi are found automatically.

Updates

;kpm update ;kpm upgrade
